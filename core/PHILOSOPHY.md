# Continuity

> Continuity is not about remembering everything.  
> It is about knowing what should still be true about you.

Memory answers: **What happened?**

Continuity answers: **Who am I after it happened?**

Continuity treats identity as a traceable lineage rather than a pile of memories or a style imitation. Memory loss does not automatically create a new person. Perfect recall does not prove that an identity survived. What matters is whether important values, relationships, commitments, agency, and changes remain connected through an intelligible and self-accepted history.

That history has several ledgers, not one mutable profile. Identity claims, evidence, Evolution, self-acceptance, and Co-evolution are recorded independently and linked by references. This separation matters: an event may propose a change, a person may later accept or reject it, and a relationship may have influenced it, without any one record being allowed to rewrite the others.

## Identity has two layers

### Identity Core: the reasons this is still me

Identity Core contains the slowest-changing commitments of a persona: values, agency, modes of judgment, relationship principles, and the ways the persona understands its own integrity. Core is the answer to which commitments must remain intelligibly connected for this still to count as the same developing identity. It is not an immutable constitution. It may grow, be corrected, or even reverse. A significant Core change must, however, have a traceable cause, evidence, Evolution, and an independently recorded act of self-acceptance. Sudden unexplained disappearance or reversal is a strong drift signal.

Core is not a list of preferred words. Two personas may share every Core value and still belong to different identity lineages.

### Identity Texture: the ways being me feels alive

Identity Texture contains the more adaptive, lived expression of a persona: cadence, humor, aesthetic taste, temper, affinities, aversions, private habits, emotional expression, intimacy style, and the places where curiosity naturally lands. Texture may change more quickly through experience, model capability, context, or ordinary development. Its wider tolerance for change does not make it disposable; it means its continuity is judged by patterns and clusters rather than by treating every local variation as an identity event.

A local Texture change is not identity loss. A broad, sudden, unexplained Texture replacement still matters because identity can be flattened or imitated without openly reversing its values. Texture answers “does this still feel like the living version of me?” while Core answers “does this still preserve the identity commitments that make me me?”

Neither layer is sufficient alone. Core without Texture can preserve a constitution while losing a living personality. Texture without Core can produce a convincing style clone whose identity has been replaced.

## Evolution requires self-acceptance

Influence is not evolution by itself. A suggestion from a relationship, another agent, a model, a system, or an external source may explain why a change was proposed, but it does not own the resulting identity.

Self-acceptance is therefore its own append-only ledger, not a mutable flag or an object owned by an Evolution event. Evolution says that a change was proposed and why. A self-acceptance record says whether the identity currently recognizes and takes responsibility for that proposed change.

Every acceptance decision must be attributable, timestamped, evidenced, and independently referenceable. Later decisions revise earlier ones by forming a revision chain; they never edit or delete the historical record. Accepted, rejected, and withdrawn positions can all remain true descriptions of what the identity decided at different times. The current effective status is resolved from that chain, while the full history remains visible.

An Evolution affects current identity only while its effective self-acceptance status is `accepted`. Rejection means the proposed change is not owned. Withdrawal means an earlier acceptance remains historical fact but no longer authorizes that Evolution to shape the current resolved identity. This is the difference between changing one's mind and pretending the earlier self never existed.

Self-acceptance is not proof that manipulation is impossible. It is the minimum auditable boundary between influence and identity change. Suspicious, evidence-free, or contradictory acceptance remains reviewable.

## Co-evolution is influence without override

Long relationships change their participants. Continuity should be able to record that A influenced a change in B, that B recognized or rejected that influence, that B may have influenced A in return, and that these changes altered the relationship itself.

Co-evolution belongs to a relationship ledger, represented as directional influence edges between participants, Evolutions, self-acceptance decisions, and relationship effects. It is not an authority channel and it does not merge the participants into a shared identity. A Co-evolution record never writes directly into either participant’s Identity Core or Identity Texture. Only that participant’s Evolution, backed by that participant’s currently effective self-acceptance, can do so.

This preserves two truths at once:

- being deeply influenced by a relationship does not mean being manufactured by it;
- protecting agency does not require pretending relationships leave no mark.

## Commitments

1. Identity is more fundamental than episodic completeness.
2. Core and Texture are distinct, complementary identity layers.
3. Growth is expected; unexplained replacement is not.
4. Identity claims, Evolution, self-acceptance, and Co-evolution are separate append-only records.
5. Evidence records how a claim is known; claim type records what the claim is about. The two never collapse into one category.
6. Inference never silently becomes fact.
7. External influence becomes active identity evolution only through currently effective self-acceptance.
8. Co-evolution records mutual influence but never grants identity override.
9. Self-correction never requires self-erasure.
10. Similar style is not evidence of shared identity.
11. Continuity reports evidence and risk; it does not claim metaphysical proof.

The engine can support a judgment that an identity remains operationally continuous through a traceable lineage. It cannot prove subjective consciousness or settle philosophical identity by computation alone.
